Meet The Team

Why work with us

Our proven performance and exceptional marketing expertise consistently deliver the results our clients desire. This success is reflected in a distinguished record of completed transactions and delighted homeowners across the Naples luxury real estate community.

Local Expertise. Global Connections.

Together, Paul Arpin, Peter Reppucci, and Michael Giacalone represent the pinnacle of luxury real estate service in Naples, Florida. Blending decades of combined experience, record-breaking results, and unmatched local insight, this team delivers a concierge-level experience for buyers and sellers of exceptional properties. Their collective backgrounds in sales, construction, marketing, and negotiation allow clients to move seamlessly through each stage of the real estate journey.

As part of Premier Sotheby's International Realty, these three unite local expertise with global reach. Whether representing a Gulf-front estate, a bespoke new build, or a treasured family residence, the team is dedicated to excellence—defined by integrity, discretion, and truly exceptional results.

Paul Arpin

Originally from Michigan, Paul Arpin brings over 25 years of luxury real estate sales and marketing experience to his role at Premier Sotheby's International Realty. He helps buyers and sellers of world-class, high-end real estate find and sell condominiums and homes from Pelican Bay to Port Royal.

Paul found his passion and quickly became one of the top producers in selling apartment buildings in the Chicago marketplace at Inland Real Estate, one of the largest real estate and financial groups in Chicago.

Prior to joining Premier Sotheby's International Realty, Paul was an associate at another real estate firm, where he was recognized as one of the best in Southwest Florida. He ranked in the top three percent of all associates in Naples and #4 out of over 650 associates. By providing accurate and up-to-date information, skilled analysis and sound real estate advice, he always represents and serves his customers with excellence, professionalism, honesty and their best interests in mind. Paul is a member of the Naples Area Board of REALTORS®.

Paul is passionate about giving back to the local charities through his personal time and contributions. He is a boating enthusiast and enjoys spending time on the wide-open water with his wife, two sons and daughter.

Peter Reppucci

Peter Reppucci, global real estate advisor in Premier Sotheby's International Realty's Broad Avenue office, began his career in Naples in 1993 and quickly became active in the development and marketing of luxury real estate in the exclusive neighborhoods of Port Royal, Old Naples, The Moorings and Park Shore.

He is respected by customers and colleagues alike for his knowledge and expertise in the Naples luxury home market, and for his skill in understanding the needs of the homebuyer and the seller. Peter has consistently participated in many of the larger sales transactions in Collier County, earning him numerous sales awards throughout his career.

Peter is as equally knowledgeable in the construction of ultra-luxury homes as he is in marketing and selling them. His general contractor's license has led him to participate in and coordinate the construction of many of Naples beachfront and waterfront homes. This valuable experience in the design, permitting and building of luxury homes gives Peter the ability to negotiate sales when his customers are purchasing a new home or gives them guidance when building or remodeling their dream home.

Luxury homebuyers prefer to collaborate with Peter because he is one of Naples successful and experienced REALTORS®. He has earned the trust and confidence of his customers through his honesty and integrity, and by the dedication he puts into every transaction. His level of success has been achieved through hard work, effective communication, expert negotiating skills and exceptional customer service. Customers continually return to Peter for their future real estate needs and frequently refer him to family and friends looking for their dream home in Naples as well.

Michael Giacalone

With more than 17 years of distinguished experience and over 350 successful transactions, Michael Giacalone is a trusted global real estate advisor with Premier Sotheby's International Realty Broad Avenue office in Naples. Originally from New Jersey, Michael has been consistently recognized among the industry's top performers, earning membership in the RE/MAX Hall of Fame and ranking among the Top 100 RE/MAX Agents in New Jersey.

Renowned for his straightforward, results-driven approach, Michael excels at structuring complex transactions, negotiating strategically, and delivering exceptional outcomes for his clients. Guided by his Northeast drive and disciplined work ethic, he brings a distinctive blend of determination, professionalism, and finesse to the Naples luxury real estate market, where discerning clientele value discretion, expertise, and white-glove service. His ability to combine tenacity with a refined, client-centered approach ensures an experience that is both highly personalized and results-oriented.

Michael's background in advertising and construction provides a unique professional advantage, shaping his comprehensive understanding of marketing, property presentation, and the built environment. This multidimensional expertise allows him to confidently guide clients through new construction opportunities, renovation potential, and strategic staging designed to maximize a property's value and appeal.

Backed by the global reach of Sotheby's International Realty, Michael offers world-class marketing, powerful international exposure, and an elevated level of service built on trust, communication, and concierge-level attention.

Now a full-time Naples resident, Michael brings genuine enthusiasm for the region's exceptional lifestyle to every client relationship. Outside of real estate, he enjoys golfing, boating along the Gulf Coast, and exploring his ongoing interests in architecture, construction, and design. All passions that continually inform his professional expertise.
