Port Royal Club, A Fresh Start for an Icon

Port Royal Club, A Fresh Start for an Icon

Michael Giacalone 5 min read
Back to Insights

The Port Royal Club here in Naples is entering a completely new chapter—and it’s a big one. If you’ve spent any time around Port Royal, you already know how special this club is. It’s not just a beach club, it’s part of the lifestyle that makes this community so unique.

Now, after Hurricane Ian in 2022 caused significant damage, the club is being rebuilt from the ground up with a $100 million vision that’s set to take things to an entirely new level.

Rather than patching things back together, members chose to start fresh. The goal is simple: create something stronger, more beautiful, and better suited for the future, while still preserving the character that people love about Port Royal.

What’s Coming

The new design is centered around the beach and the outdoor experience, with thoughtful upgrades throughout:

A 62,000-square-foot clubhouse designed to fully capture Gulf views

Open green spaces and gardens that make it easy to enjoy being outside

Several dining options, from casual to more refined, both indoors and out

A beachfront pool and spa area

A reimagined beach setup with cabanas, umbrellas, and plenty of seating

A wine cellar with private member lockers

An event space for up to 150 guests

Expanded parking (which, honestly, will be a welcome upgrade)

Landscaping designed for both privacy and a quieter, more relaxed feel

A West Indies–inspired architectural style that fits perfectly with Naples

The team behind it includes some of the best in the business, and everything is currently on track for a reopening sometime in 2026.

Why This Club Is So Special

One of the things that makes the Port Royal Club different is that it’s only available to Port Royal homeowners. With fewer than 700 members, it stays private, low-key, and incredibly well curated.

It’s the kind of place where you can spend the entire day without ever needing to leave—whether that means the beach, lunch with friends, a workout, or dinner overlooking the Gulf.

Members have access to:

A private beach and clubhouse

Gulf-front swimming and lounging

Fitness and spa services

Dining options for any mood

Tennis and social events

A really strong sense of community

What This Means for Port Royal Real Estate

From a real estate perspective, this rebuild matters—a lot.

A brand-new, world-class beach club adds another layer of appeal to an already top-tier neighborhood. Buyers at this level aren’t just looking at homes, they’re looking at lifestyle, and the Port Royal Club is a big part of that equation.

It also sends a clear message: this community is investing in its future, and that kind of long-term commitment tends to support property values over time.

Timeline

Expected reopening: sometime in 2026

Final Thoughts

This isn’t just a rebuild—it feels more like a reset and a reimagining of what the Port Royal Club can be. It’s exciting to see something so iconic being brought back in a way that respects its history but also raises the bar.
