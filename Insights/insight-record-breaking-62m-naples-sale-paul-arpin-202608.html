Record-breaking sale in Naples Florida

In 2022, Paul Arpin of Premier Sotheby’s International Realty orchestrated a landmark transaction in Naples real estate. He represented both the buyer and seller in the $62 million sale of a more than 16,000-square-foot beachfront estate at 25 16th Ave. S., setting a new record for single-family residential properties in the area. This sale surpassed the previous record by $10 million.The six-bedroom estate, situated on 1.5 acres off Gulf Shore Boulevard, was a collaborative creation by Naples-based professionals: designed by Randall Stofft and John Cooney of Stofft Cooney Architects, built by Dave Rogers of Newbury North Associates Inc., and interior-designed by Jeffrey Strasser from Marc-Michaels Interior Design. The property features lush landscaping by Koby Kirwin, a first-floor pool, a second-floor infinity-edge pool with panoramic Gulf views, an exercise wing over a three-car garage, and two motor courts .gulfshorebusiness.comPaul Arpin's role in this historic sale underscores his expertise and prominence in Naples' luxury real estate market.
